Welcome to The Page to Screen Archive!

This database of book-to-screen adaptations will be kept up to date as soon as I physically can. Feel free to browse the archives, and if you notice anything that needs updating, please reach out. Information about international adaptations is greatly appreciated.

The Mission

The inspiration for The Page to Screen project came from a simple realization that book lovers everywhere were constantly searching for a complete, up-to-date tracker of page-to-screen adaptations. With new adaptations announced daily, finding a single, reliable list was nearly impossible. The only option would be to navigate many different resources daily to stay informed. The Page to Screen Archive's mission is to make tracking upcoming movies and TV shows simple, organized, and fun for everyone through one simple, interactive digital archive.

    How to Navigate the Database

    There are a few pages on the website, so here is what each page is for:

    • ▶️ Upcoming Adaptations: A structured schedule of upcoming releases locked in with official launch dates.
    • 🎬 Anticipated Adaptations: A list of shows and movies newly announced, in production, filming, and projects that don't have official release dates yet.
    • 📥 Submission Form: Your way to contribute! Spot an unlisted adaptation announcement? Drop the details here to help grow our archive tracker.
    • 🪙 Support the Project: This is a free resource. If you find it useful and are able to, a donation page is available. Any and all of your contributions help us keep the archive up to date and accessible to everyone.

    The Database

    There is a lot of data about these upcoming movies and TV series that may be helpful to you:

    • Title: The title of the upcoming adaptation.
    • Author: The author who wrote the original book.
    • Format: Movie or TV!
    • Release Date: The date it will be released.
    • Stages: In Development, Pre-Production, Filming, or Post-Production.
    • Guidance Rating: Helpful to know if it's family-friendly or mature content.
    • Network/Studio: Tells you if the adaptation will be in theaters or on a streaming service.
    • Genre: Narrow down the database by searching your favorite genre.
